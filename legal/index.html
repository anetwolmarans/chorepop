Terms, Privacy & Refunds

The short, honest version — written for families, not lawyers.

🎁What ChorePop costs

ChorePop is completely free — unlimited chores and unlimited family members for everyone. There is no trial to run out and nothing to unlock.

If paid plans are ever introduced (for example through Google Play), this page will be updated first — before anyone is asked to pay.

🛡️Payments

ChorePop does not take payments and never sees card details. If a paid plan is introduced one day, it will be handled by the app store or payment provider you buy it through — your card data would live on their secure page only, never inside ChorePop.

↩️Refund policy

There is nothing to charge or refund while ChorePop is free. If a paid plan is ever introduced and a charge goes wrong, tell us within 14 days and we'll refund it in full, no interrogation.

🔒Privacy — what we store

ChorePop stores only what your family types in: the family name, the first names and emoji avatars you add, and the chores and completion history. Signing in is anonymous — the app never asks for an email or password.

The data lives in Google's Firebase service. We don't sell it, don't show ads and don't add tracking. Want it gone? Message us and we'll delete your family's data for good.

ChorePop is set up by a parent or guardian. We never collect personal information directly from children — only the first name and emoji a parent chooses to enter.

📜Terms of service

ChorePop is provided as-is. You're responsible for what your family enters. We may improve or change features over time. If a paid plan is ever introduced, its price, renewal and cancellation terms will appear on this page before you are asked to pay. Be kind to your family — that's genuinely the point of the app.

📮Contact

The fastest way to reach the developer is the public project page: github.com/anetwolmarans/chorepop — open an issue there for refunds, data deletion or anything else, and you'll get a real answer.
